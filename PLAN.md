# The 12-week plan

The mistake most people make is trying to fix everything on Monday, burning out by Thursday and giving up. This plan adds **one layer every few weeks**. Each layer should feel almost too easy. That's on purpose: you're building the habit of showing up, not proving anything.

> **Rules for the whole 12 weeks**
> 1. **Make it tiny.** If you don't feel like it, do the 2-minute version (shave the neckline only, walk to the end of the street, send one message).
> 2. **Never miss twice.** Missing one day is normal. Missing two days is how you start a new habit of not doing it.
> 3. **Schedule it or it doesn't exist.** Social plans, gym and grooming go into your calendar like a class.
> 4. **Use Claude for anything that makes you stall.** If a task has sat untouched for 3 days, press "Break into steps".

---

## Weeks 1–2: Foundation (look after the basics)

Goal: get the daily basics going, and see clearly where your time goes.

- [ ] Fill in your real hours in the **Week** tab and see your actual free time.
- [ ] Set the "last done" date for every item in the **Style → Upkeep** schedule.
- [ ] Do the morning basics every day: shower, deodorant, face wash, moisturizer, teeth.
- [ ] **Get a haircut.** Use the "Haircut to ask for" request in Claude first so you know what to say to the barber. Book the next one before you leave.
- [ ] Shave or trim to a clean line. Pick one look (clean-shaven, stubble or short beard) and keep it up.
- [ ] Clean your room once, properly. After that, 10 minutes a day keeps it that way.
- [ ] Social: **Level 1** of the ladder (eye contact, small talk with cashiers, reply to group chats).

## Weeks 3–4: Clothes (audit + plan)

Goal: know what you own, get rid of what doesn't work, and plan what to buy.

- [ ] Take everything out of your closet. Keep only what fits, isn't damaged, and you'd actually wear. Donate or sell the rest.
- [ ] Tick off what you already own in **Style → Capsule wardrobe**. Add the missing items to the wishlist.
- [ ] Enter your income and fixed costs in **Budget**. Set a monthly upgrade fund (the app suggests an amount).
- [ ] Ask Claude for "Outfits from what I own". You probably have more outfits than you think.
- [ ] Lay out tomorrow's outfit every night (it's already a daily habit in the app).
- [ ] Social: **Level 2**. Ask a classmate a question, give one genuine compliment.

## Weeks 5–8: Getting out

Goal: something social in your calendar every week, and some movement.

- [ ] Join **one** thing that repeats weekly: a uni club, a sports team, a gym class, volunteering. Repeating it is the point: the same people every week is how friendships happen.
- [ ] 2 to 3 short workouts or long walks per week. Exercise is the cheapest way to look and feel better.
- [ ] First fragrance: get samples and decants, wear each one for a full day, then buy one.
- [ ] Social: **Level 3**. Invite a classmate for coffee, message an old friend, go to one event.
- [ ] Buy wishlist items as they become "ready to buy". Fit first: get trousers hemmed if they bunch at the ankle.

## Weeks 9–12: Building on it

Goal: turn this into normal life.

- [ ] Social: **Levels 4–5**. Organise something small, see the same people a second time, try a solo outing.
- [ ] Say yes to every reasonable invite for a month.
- [ ] Try one new thing a week: a new café, an exhibition, a class.
- [ ] Review: what's working, what's not, what to drop. Ask Claude to review your 12 weeks with you.

## The weekly loop (keep this forever)

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Every morning | Basics: shower, skin, teeth, grooming check | 10 min |
| Every night | Lay out outfit, 2-minute plan for tomorrow, 10-minute tidy | 15 min |
| Sunday | Weekly reset: schedule, laundry, upkeep, tasks, social plans | 30 min |
| Payday | Press Payday in the Budget tab, buy only what's ready | 5 min |
| Every 4 weeks | Haircut (pre-booked) | |

## If you fall off

You will fall off at some point. Everyone does. When it happens:

1. Don't try to catch up. Start again from today.
2. Do only the 3 easiest habits for 3 days.
3. Then add things back one at a time.
