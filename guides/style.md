# Style: dressing better without thinking about it

## The 3 rules
1. **Fit beats everything.** A €15 T-shirt that fits looks better than a €100 one that doesn't.
2. **Fewer colours.** Navy, grey, white, black, beige, olive. They all go together, so everything matches.
3. **Basics first, statement pieces later.** Build a base of plain items before buying anything loud.

## How things should fit
| Item | Fit check |
|---|---|
| T-shirt | Shoulder seam on your shoulder bone. Sleeve hits mid-bicep. Hem covers your belt, no more. |
| Shirt | You can button the collar without it pulling. No fabric ballooning at the waist. |
| Jeans / chinos | Slim or straight, not skinny, not baggy. Little or no bunching at the ankle. Get them hemmed if needed (cheap and worth it). |
| Jacket | Shoulders end where yours do. You can cross your arms without strain. |
| Shoes | Clean. Clean shoes count for more than expensive ones. |

## Capsule wardrobe (12 pieces)
3 white T-shirts · 2 navy/black T-shirts · white or light-blue oxford shirt · navy or grey crewneck sweater · dark jeans · beige or navy chinos · white leather sneakers · chelsea or desert boots · overshirt or bomber · wool coat (winter) · leather belt that matches your shoes · simple watch.

These are in the app (**Style → Capsule wardrobe**). Tick what you own and add the rest to the wishlist.

### Outfit formulas you can repeat
1. White tee + dark jeans + white sneakers (+ overshirt). The default for uni.
2. Oxford shirt + chinos + desert boots. For work, dates, family dinners.
3. Crewneck sweater over a tee + dark jeans + boots. Autumn and winter.
4. Navy tee + beige chinos + white sneakers. Summer.
5. Any of the above + wool coat. Instantly looks more put-together.

## Shopping rules
- Buy from your **wishlist only**, in priority order, when the app says "ready to buy".
- Wait 48 hours before any unplanned purchase over €30.
- Try things on. If it's online, check the size chart against a piece you own that fits well.
- Look at second-hand (Vinted and similar) for coats, boots and wool. Check the brand's sizing first.
- Every new item should go with at least 3 things you already own.

## Fragrance
- Start with **one fresh everyday scent** (citrus, aquatic, light woody). Add a warmer evening scent later.
- Test on skin, not paper strips. Wait 2–3 hours, because the scent changes.
- Buy **samples or decants** (5–10 ml) before a full bottle. They're cheap and stop expensive mistakes.
- 2–4 sprays on the neck and chest. Don't rub your wrists together.
- Fragrance goes on top of a shower and clean clothes, never instead of them.
