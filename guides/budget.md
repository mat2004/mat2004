# Money: budgeting for clothes, grooming and fragrance

## The system: pay yourself an "upgrade fund"
Each month, when you get paid:

1. **Fixed costs first:** rent/board, food, transport, phone, subscriptions.
2. **Savings:** build an emergency fund equal to **one month of fixed costs**, then keep adding a little.
3. **Upgrade fund:** a fixed amount for clothes, grooming and fragrance. A good starting point is **about 15% of what's left after fixed costs.** The app works this out for you.
4. **Spending money:** whatever is left is free to spend. The app shows it per week too.

In the app: enter these in **Budget**, add items to the **Wishlist** with a priority, and press **Payday** each month. The fund goes into the top-priority item first, and every item shows the month you'll be able to afford it.

## Example (replace with your numbers)
| | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Net income | €900 |
| Fixed costs | €450 |
| Savings | €100 |
| Upgrade fund | €70 |
| Free spending | €280 (about €65 a week) |

With €70 a month: white sneakers (€90) in month 2, dark jeans (€60) in month 3, first fragrance decants (€20) in month 3…

## Rules
- **Never buy clothes or fragrance on credit or Klarna-style pay-later.**
- Buy in priority order. "Must" items are the ones that improve the most outfits (shoes, jeans, a good jacket).
- Wait 48 hours before any unplanned purchase over €30.
- Before buying, ask: does it go with 3 things I already own?
- Check second-hand first for coats, boots and knitwear.
- Cancel one subscription you don't use, and put that money into the upgrade fund.
