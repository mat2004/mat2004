# Using Claude as your assistant

Claude can do a lot of the thinking, planning and writing you've been putting off. Some ways to use it:

- **In the app:** every task has **Break into steps** (asks Claude right inside the app) and **Open in Claude** (opens a new chat with the request filled in).
- **In this repo with Claude Code:** `CLAUDE.md` makes Claude act as your coach. Try: *"Coach me through today"*, *"I finished week 2, tick it off and tell me what's next"*, *"Log my weekly review"*.
- **On claude.ai or the Claude app:** copy any request below.

Tip: make a **Project** on claude.ai called "Glow-Up" and paste the "About me" part of `CLAUDE.md` into its instructions. Then every chat in it already knows your situation.

---

## Requests you can copy

### Daily / weekly
> Here's my week: [work hours, class times, commute]. I want to fit in grooming, 2–3 workouts, 2 social things and rest. Build a realistic schedule with specific days and times. Ask me questions first if anything is missing.

> I've been avoiding this task for days: [task]. Break it into steps of 10 minutes or less, and tell me the first 2-minute action. Tell me which parts you can do for me.

> I fell off my routine for [X days]. Restart me gently: give me the 3 easiest things to do today and a plan for the next 3 days.

### Grooming
> Build me a men's grooming routine under 10 minutes a day. Ask about my skin type, beard and hair first. Give product *types*, the order of steps, and a weekly/monthly schedule.

> Help me pick a haircut. Ask me about face shape, hair type and how much styling time I want. Then tell me exactly what to say to the barber and how to style it in 2 minutes.

### Style
> Here's what I own: [list]. Build me 7 outfits for uni, work and going out. Tell me the 3 items to buy next that would add the most new outfits.

> I want to buy [item] for about [budget]. What should I look for in fit, material and colour? Give me options at different price points, including second-hand.

> Help me choose my first fragrances (one fresh daily, one evening) on a student budget. Ask what I like first. Explain how to test them and where to buy decants.

### Social
> I want to invite [person / classmate / coworker] to [coffee / lunch / event]. Write 3 short, natural messages that are easy to say yes or no to.

> I'm going to [event] and I'll know almost nobody. Give me 5 openers, how to join a group that's already talking, and how to leave a conversation politely.

> Help me reply to this group chat I've ignored for a while: [paste]. Keep it casual, no big apology.

### Uni + work
> Make a study plan for my [subject] exam on [date]. Topics: [list]. I have about [X] hours a day. Use short sessions and spaced repetition, and include rest days.

> Explain [concept] like I'm smart but missed the lecture, then quiz me with 5 questions.

> Help me write an email to my [professor / manager] about [situation]. Polite and short.

### Money
> My monthly income is [X]. Fixed costs: [list]. I want an emergency fund and a monthly upgrade fund for clothes, grooming and fragrance. Tell me realistic amounts, what to cut, and the order to buy my wishlist in: [list].
