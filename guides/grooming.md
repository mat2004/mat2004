# Grooming: the 10-minute routine

## Every day
**Morning (5 min)**
1. Shower (a quick one is fine) and use deodorant.
2. Face: gentle face wash, then moisturizer **with SPF 30+**. SPF is the single best thing for how your skin looks in 10 years.
3. Teeth. Hair product if you use it: a pea-sized amount of matte clay, rubbed between your palms, worked in from the back to the front.

**Evening (3 min)**
1. Face wash + moisturizer (no SPF needed).
2. Brush teeth + floss.

## Facial hair: pick one look and keep it up
| Look | Upkeep |
|---|---|
| Clean-shaven | Shave every 1–2 days. Shave after a shower, with the grain first. |
| Stubble | Trimmer on a 1–3 mm guard every 2–3 days. Clean up the neckline. |
| Short beard | Trim every week. **Neckline:** about two fingers above your Adam's apple. **Cheek line:** keep it natural, just remove strays. |

A clean neckline matters more than anything else. A messy neck looks lazy, even on a good beard.

## Haircut
- Every **3–4 weeks** for short styles, 5–6 for longer ones.
- **Book the next one before you leave the barber.** This is the whole trick.
- Not sure what to ask for? Use the "Haircut to ask for" request in [claude-prompts.md](claude-prompts.md), and save a photo of a cut you like to show the barber.

## Small things people notice
- Nails short and clean (every 10 days).
- Eyebrows: only remove the hairs in the middle and the obvious strays. Never reshape the whole brow.
- Nose and ear hair: a cheap detail trimmer, every 3 weeks.
- Lips: lip balm in winter.
- Breath: drink water, brush your tongue.
- Clothes: clean, not wrinkled, no lint. This counts as grooming too.
